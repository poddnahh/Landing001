Questions

Is it really just $10?

Yes. One payment, lifetime access. Optional add-on packs are one-time too. No subscriptions.

My parent isn't good with technology. Can I record for them?

Absolutely. Add them to your tree, then sit together and record their answers on your phone — pick their name under "Whose memory is this?" and it goes into their memory book.

What happens to the memories if I stop paying or lose my phone?

You never lose access to what you've saved — viewing and exporting memory books is always free. Use "Backup & share family file" regularly to keep a copy and share it with family.

How does my family see what I share?

Everyone can use one shared device with their own profile, or you can send the family file to relatives who import it on their own phones. Real-time cloud sync is on the way.
